Frequently asked questions

What is Adaptive Relative Strength (ARS)? +

ARS measures a stock's cumulative outperformance relative to the Nifty 50, anchored since January 1, 2021. A stock up 200% while Nifty gained 50% shows the true alpha generated over the benchmark.

How often is the data refreshed? +

An automated End-of-Day pipeline runs via GitHub Actions every trading day at 7:00 PM IST, pulling official NSE Bhavcopy and Yahoo Finance series. You can also trigger on-demand recalculations during market hours.

Is Adaptive Alpha completely free? +

Yes — 100% free and open-access. No paywalls, hidden fees, or mandatory sign-ups to use the terminal.

How does SRS differ from standard RSI? +

RSI measures a single stock's internal price momentum on a 0–100 oscillator. SRS measures comparative outperformance against the Nifty 50 over a rolling 63-day (one business quarter) window, identifying true market leadership rather than isolated momentum.
