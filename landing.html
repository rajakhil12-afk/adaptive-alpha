Frequently Asked Questions

What is Adaptive Relative Strength (ARS)? +
ARS measures a stock's cumulative outperformance relative to the NIFTY 50 index anchored since January 1, 2021. If a stock gained 200% while Nifty gained 50%, ARS reflects true alpha generated over benchmark performance.
How often is the data refreshed? +
The screener runs an automated End-of-Day (EOD) data pipeline via GitHub Actions every trading day at 7:00 PM IST, pulling official NSE Bhavcopy and Yahoo Finance series. You can also trigger on-demand live recalculations during market hours directly in the terminal.
Is Adaptive Alpha completely free? +
Yes! Adaptive Alpha is 100% free and open-access. There are no paywalls, hidden fees, or mandatory sign-ups required to use the terminal.
How does SRS differ from standard RSI? +
RSI (Relative Strength Index) only measures internal price momentum of a single stock on a 0-100 oscillator. SRS (Shorter-term Relative Strength) measures comparative outperformance against the NIFTY 50 benchmark over a 63-day (1 business quarter) rolling window, identifying true market leadership.
