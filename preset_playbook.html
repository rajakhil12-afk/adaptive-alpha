1. 🌟 Preset: Dual Alpha Leaders (Quad 1)

Philosophy: Buy established, high-velocity market leaders that possess both long-term structural tailwinds and active short-term quarterly acceleration. This is the flagship momentum setup with the highest statistical win-rate.

✅ ENTRY RULES

  • Preset Condition: Stock must be in Quad 1 (Both ARS > 0% and SRS > 0%).
  • Trend Confirmation: Supertrend must be in BUY status and stock must be MA+ (above 50MA & 200MA).
  • Trigger Event: Enter on a fresh *NEW* ARS crossover or when volume exceeds 1.3x average volume.

🛑 EXIT & STOP-LOSS RULES

  • Initial Stop-Loss: Set hard stop at the Supertrend (ST14) Price Level (auto-calculated in modal).
  • Profit Target 1 (1:2 R:R): Sell 50% position when price reaches Target 1 (e.g. +8% to +12% gain). Move stop-loss to breakeven.
  • Profit Target 2 (1:3 R:R): Trail the remaining 50% position along the Supertrend line until a SELL signal fires.

⚠️ WHAT TO AVOID / RED FLAGS

  • Avoid Extended Entries: Do not buy if the stock is already > 25% above its 50MA (overextended).
  • Avoid Low Volume Breakouts: Skip breakouts accompanied by volume ratio < 1.0x (lacks institutional backing).

✅ ENTRY RULES

🛑 EXIT & STOP-LOSS RULES

  • Profit Target 1: Take 50% profit at +10% gain (near overhead resistance).
  • Trailing Exit: Trail the remaining 50% using ST10 Supertrend line.

⚠️ WHAT TO AVOID / RED FLAGS

  • Avoid Below 200MA: Skip stocks trading far below their 200-day moving average (heavy overhead supply).
  • Avoid Weak SRS: Skip if SRS is barely positive (< +1.0%). Look for strong SRS acceleration.
